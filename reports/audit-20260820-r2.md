## Findings

### MAJOR-1 - CAPTURE accepted an unreported fallback state (FIXED, `deac4ec`)

The CAPTURE producer normalized a missing/non-boolean `GPUAdapter.isFallbackAdapter` value to
`null`, then rejected only literal `true`. Its positive Linux fixture deliberately used `null`.
Both the profile-union consumer and APPLY finalizer made the same allowance. A named NVIDIA
identity with no browser-reported fallback status therefore became `ACCEPTED`, contrary to the
runbook's requirement for a browser-reported non-fallback adapter.

The producer now classifies every value other than literal `false` as
`fallback-status-absent`; both independent consumers require exact `False`; and the adversarial
matrix carries the missing-status mutation. Browser-free replay is 13 positive / 23 negative in
the producer and 4 positive / 29 negative across both consumers
(`ledger/buildlogs/20260820T232116-2638257.log`). No CAPTURE receipt existed, so no accepted
profile was invalidated.

### MAJOR-2 - the M8 consumer ignored the recorded REUSE identity (FIXED, `deac4ec`)

The compliance producer recorded the selected REUSE executable's path, version, size, and
SHA-256, but `check_compliance()` consumed none of those fields. A minimal receipt with
`/forged/reuse`, version `0`, one byte, and an all-zero digest returned zero verifier failures.
The identity was ornamental evidence rather than a gate input.

The independent M8 verifier now requires the exact four-field record, rejects symlinked,
non-canonical, missing, or non-executable paths, reruns the exact `reuse, version 6.2.0` check,
rehashes the live executable, and compares the complete record. Its self-check covers a live
temporary executable plus a forged digest; the full M8 consumer suite is green
(`ledger/buildlogs/20260820T232148-2638488.log`). The current compliance receipt is correctly
stale after source/history changes and must be regenerated during a future M8 candidate.

### MAJOR-3 - Linux browser receipts do not bind their runtime adapter (OPEN)

Seven Linux-ported live producers launch a browser but never query or record the adapter:
`verify_browser_usd.mjs`, `verify_files.mjs`, `browser_matrix.mjs`,
`verify_product_bar.mjs`, `measure_current.mjs`, `soak_current.mjs`, and
`verify_staged.mjs`. They rely on operator sequencing and possession of an APPLY artifact.
A valid hardware-profiled APPLY product could therefore be replayed later on llvmpipe or
SwiftShader and yield a lane receipt without proving the runtime adapter used for that lane.

No current claim is invalidated: all seven live receipts are absent and their gates are RED.
The queued repair is one shared exact adapter contract, required before evidence allocation and
independently consumed by M5-M8 receipt verifiers. Its synthetic rejection matrix is unblocked;
hardware acceptance and live receipts remain blocked by s7.
