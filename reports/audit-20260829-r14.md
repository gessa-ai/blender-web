## High findings

### 1. The Apple sparse receipt compared non-adjacent frames (repaired)

`rapid_freeze_repro.mjs` and `analyze_sparse_hardware_series.py` compared the pending-selection
navigation frame and the completed transform frame to the older pre-click orbit baseline. A click
redraw could therefore make the navigation hash differ while the requested orbit remained stale;
the final transform could then equal that stale navigation frame and still differ from the older
baseline. Native state advancing did not prove that either action reached pixels.

A fail-first corruption setting both frames stale was accepted by the predecessor consumer
(`ledger/buildlogs/20260829T212327-353791.log`). The producer now requires
selection-pending -> navigation pixels/native view/rotate retirement, then uses navigation as the
terminal drain's pixel baseline. The independent consumer repeats both adjacent comparisons and
the navigation native-view comparison. Its synthetic fixtures and mutations bind all three.
The corrected corruption is rejected at `ledger/buildlogs/20260829T212644-355816.log`; the final
consumer, 91-mutation producer/source verifier, and composed-gauntlet self-checks are green at
`20260829T212623-355591.log`, `20260829T212644-355815.log`, and
`20260829T212644-355841.log`.

### 2. Selection failure can retain ordinary input for 30 seconds (open)

`af966ec` replaced an approximately 2.4-second timer-event ceiling with a 30-second wall-clock
ceiling in `patches/0314-view3d-web-selection-wall-timeout.patch`. Wall time is the correct clock,
but the new duration is 12.5 times the nominal old bound. During a failed pick the modal retains
ordinary state-changing input, so a real failure can look like the filed freeze for 30 seconds.
The current source check mirrors the literal duration; it does not prove timely cancellation,
FIFO replay, redraw recovery, or a usable product after timeout. The producer's separate 12-second
Apple evidence ceiling does not constrain shipping behavior.

Required repair: derive a bounded product timeout from measured conformant-hardware selection
latency, then prove timeout cancellation replays or safely cancels retained input and the next
isolated action changes native state and pixels. Do not merely shorten the test model.

## Medium findings

### 3. Two sibling contracts encoded the retired intermediate-drain flow (repaired)

The fixed-cadence producer deliberately samples click and navigation without an intermediate
`waitForActionDrain`, then drains once after the retained transform tail. The navigation and stream
continuation source contracts still required the prior click/nav drain shape and failed against
committed HEAD (`ledger/buildlogs/20260829T212900-357949.log` and
`20260829T212900-357960.log`). They now require both fixed-cadence samples, forbid an intermediate
drain, require the terminal drain to use the navigation frame, and mutate the sampling call back to
the retired shape. Final self-checks pass at `20260829T212951-358601.log` and
`20260829T213002-358759.log`.

### 4. The sparse freeze receipt is not a zero-artifact receipt (open boundary)

The producer records draw-drop generations, selection validation failures, and GPU diagnostic
lines, but the sparse consumer is intentionally a liveness discriminator: it proves adjacent
actions change pixels, not that every golden-path region matches a structural reference. Internal
first-use drops may retry successfully, while a hash inequality alone cannot exclude white/black/
grey shapes, clipped glyphs, or missing geometry. Therefore a sparse 10/10 PASS must never close
P0-I/J alone. The existing same-generation zero-artifact/interaction gauntlet remains mandatory;
future composition should also bind the recorded failure census explicitly.
