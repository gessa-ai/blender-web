# Stuck record - 2026-08-24

## Trigger

More than three consecutive M8 registration-cut iterations changed product size without changing
the required harness result: M8 remained 0/1 red. The accepted Sculpt/Paint, Grease Pencil,
Compositor, VSE, Spreadsheet, Clip, NLA, and Physics cuts saved 1,254,866 Brotli-q11 bytes in
total, but the final 23,471,033-byte Wasm alone remains 8,471,033 bytes over the complete 15 MB
interactive budget before stage-0 data. Script and Mask candidates were rejected.

## Diagnosis

Serial registration cuts are exhausted and conflict with the standing fidelity-first decision in
`notes/decisions.md` D-10. They permanently remove native-visible browser features instead of
staging them. The compositor and editor-space cuts also cross file-preservation boundaries:
Blender converts missing compositor types to undefined nodes and missing editor spaces to
`SPACE_EMPTY`, discarding type-specific storage or regions during load.

The remaining structural route is a function-profile-driven primary/secondary Wasm split. A
truthful profile and split product require an accepted hardware WebGPU run. That is externally
blocked on this machine by **no conformant hardware Vulkan ICD in WSL2 (NVIDIA ships none; Mesa
dzn rejected by Dawn)**. A software/fallback adapter cannot bind the profile or split product.

## Recovery

1. Stop all further windowed registration cuts.
2. Reverse patches 0248-0255 and retire the eight size-only deferrals as one written, canonical
   rollback.
3. Add hardware-independent `.blend` load/save/reload parity tests for the compositor and omitted
   editor spaces so a future lazy boundary cannot destroy file state.
4. Keep M8's profile/split-product boundary deferred to the named s7 blocker; if a later accepted
   hardware profile cannot meet 15 MB, defer the performance bar rather than narrow the product.
