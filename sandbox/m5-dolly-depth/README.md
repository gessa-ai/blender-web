# M5 direct-dolly depth continuation contract

This device-free contract binds the stock direct dolly operator to the shared owned progressive-
depth continuation. It checks native-immediate and browser-pending settlement, latest-event replay,
delta and vertical/horizontal trackpad one-shots, perspective switching, motion/autokey/undo,
move/rotate modal switches, and fail-closed context drift, Escape, readback failure, and timeout.
The source verifier mutates the shipping postimage and compiles its exact translation unit with the
locked native and Wasm product commands.

This is source and state-machine evidence, not a browser, hardware-adapter, profile, split-product,
or live M5 receipt. Painting, zoom-border, and NDOF depth consumers plus the depth-cache and window-
capture families remain.
