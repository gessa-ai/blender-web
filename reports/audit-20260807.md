## Findings

### MAJOR-1 - the M3 census "held 148/158 + 956/973, twice this round" claim for 0110/0112/0113 has no committed harness receipt and no rebuilt native binary
The range's headline patches are the r26/r27 viewport fixes, which touch the **exact code
paths the GPU gate exercises**: bind-group assembly (`wgpu_shader.cc` / `wgpu_context.cc`,
patch 0112), framebuffer load-store (`wgpu_framebuffer.cc/.hh`, 0110), and indexed-draw
index start/base (`wgpu_batch.cc`, 0113). Three progress entries assert the native census
was re-run and held:
- r26 `a45038f`: *"NATIVE CENSUS RE-RUN (fixes unguarded, semantically right for native -
  must confirm 148/158+956/973)"* (landing debt).
- r27a `2dfec4c`: *"census (kicked)"*.
- r27 close `2e74628`: *"census GREEN 148/158 + static 956/973 (twice this round)"*.

The committed evidence contradicts a recorded re-run:
1. **The gate file is stale.** `ledger/results/m3.json` (committed HEAD) has
   `ts: 2026-08-07T11:39:10Z` and was last modified in-range at **r24 `cd54563`**. Its
   `patches_series` detail ends at `...0108-...:applied 0120-asset-system-wasm.patch:applied`
   and lists **none** of 0109/0110/0111/0112/0113.
2. **The native binary predates the patches.** `build-native-gpu/bin/tests/blender_test`
   mtime is **2026-08-06T15:50**; the newest webgpu object
   (`.../bf_gpu.dir/webgpu/wgpu_context.cc.o`) is also 2026-08-06T15:50; **zero** objects in
   the native tree are newer than the patch landing (0110 `2026-08-07T16:16`, 0112/0113
   `2026-08-07T17:28`). The tree cannot contain the fixes (grep for the 0112 symbol
   `slot_is_mapped` in `build-native-gpu/` is empty).
3. **Even the uncommitted regen is short of the final patches.** The working-tree (not
   committed) `m3.json` at `ts: 2026-08-07T21:27:50Z` includes 0110 and the **draft** 0112
   (`0112-gpu-webgpu-bind-remap-absent-slots`), but **not** the final
   `0112-gpu-webgpu-bind-assembly-precedence` (the two-pass rewrite that shipped) and **not**
   0113. So the shipped bind-assembly pack and index start/base fix appear in **no** m3.json,
   committed or otherwise.

The prior auditor independently re-ran 148/158 at `aec2568` (pre-0110). I could **not**
re-verify for this range: the native binary predates the patches and the native tree lacks
0112, so a relink would be required, which is out of the read-only audit scope and beyond
"if runtime permits" for a native Blender-GPU link. Net: M3 is marked DONE and the human's
gate cites a census that predates the M4-round upstream edits whose regression-safety the
driver claims but has not recorded. GOAL is explicit: *"No completion claims without harness
output."* This may well be true (the fixes are plausibly gate-neutral), but the **receipt is
missing** for the range's load-bearing patches, and a claim of a native re-run that the build
tree shows never happened is a ledger-honesty defect on the central gate. Fix: relink
`build-native-gpu` at HEAD, re-run the census, and commit the regenerated `m3.json` (with
0110/0112/0113 in `patches_series`) before the next milestone claim - or downgrade the
progress wording from "census GREEN twice this round" to the honest "native re-run pending".

### MINOR-2 - `reuse lint` is red on committed state; the "reuse lint GREEN" claim is stale (FIXED here)
`REUSE.toml:83`'s `sandbox/**` glob enumerates many extensions but omits `.obj/.mtl/.glb`,

`sandbox/m7-io-smoke/out/native/cube.{obj,mtl,glb}` and
`sandbox/m7-io-smoke/out/wasm/cube.{obj,mtl}` (added in-range at `080df00`). This contradicts
the progress claim *"reuse lint GREEN incl 3 pre-existing strays fixed"* (driver line
2026-08-07T10:55Z), which predates the M7-IO drop. **Fixed in this commit**: extended the
`REUSE.toml:83` glob with `sandbox/**/*.{obj,mtl,glb,gltf}` (the project's own
glob-the-sandbox-artifact convention); `reuse lint-file` on all five now returns clean
(verified by re-execution). Two residual, **untracked** items remain (out of scope for a
clean CI checkout, not fixed): the new build trees `build-wasm-cycles/` and
`build-wasm-windowed-opt/` are absent from `.gitignore` (continuation of audit-20260805
MINOR-1) and pollute local lint with ~thousands of artifacts; recommend adding both to
`.gitignore` to prevent an accidental mass commit.
